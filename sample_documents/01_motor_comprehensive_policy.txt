MERIDIAN GENERAL INSURANCE
MOTOR COMPREHENSIVE POLICY
Document Code: MGI-MOTOR-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026

Section 1 - Scope of Cover
This policy covers accidental physical damage to the insured vehicle caused by collision, overturning, fire, lightning, explosion, theft, malicious damage, flood, cyclone, storm, earthquake, landslide, and falling objects during the active policy period.

Section 2 - Third-Party Liability
Third-party bodily injury and property-damage liability are covered only up to the limits stated in the policy schedule and subject to applicable law.

Section 3 - Required Claim Documents
The claimant must provide the completed claim form, policy number, vehicle registration certificate, valid driving licence of the driver, repair estimate, photographs of damage, and police report when theft, injury, or major property damage is involved.

Section 4 - Exclusions
The policy does not cover normal wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown without accidental damage, intentional loss, racing, use outside the permitted purpose, driving without a valid licence, or driving under the influence of alcohol or prohibited substances.

Section 5 - Notification
The insured should report an accident within 48 hours of becoming aware of the loss. Delay must be explained and may require manager review.

Section 6 - Deductible
A standard deductible of INR 5,000 applies to each approved accidental-damage claim unless the policy schedule states a different amount.
