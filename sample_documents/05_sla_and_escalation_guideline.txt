MERIDIAN GENERAL INSURANCE
CLAIMS SLA AND ESCALATION GUIDELINE
Document Code: MGI-SLA-001

Section 1 - Acknowledgement
A submitted claim should be acknowledged within 4 business hours.

Section 2 - Initial Review
Initial document and policy validation should be completed within 24 hours of submission.

Section 3 - Standard Decision Target
A standard claim should receive an approval or rejection decision within 72 hours after all required documents are received.

Section 4 - SLA Breach
An open claim pending for more than 72 hours is flagged as an SLA breach unless it is waiting for documents and the reason is recorded.

Section 5 - Escalation
Claims approaching 60 hours without a decision should be escalated to the Claims Manager. High-value claims and repeated documentation delays should be prioritized.

Section 6 - Reporting
SLA reporting must show total open claims, breached claims, region, claim type, assigned adjuster, and claim age.
