MERIDIAN GENERAL INSURANCE
CLAIMIQ AI ANSWERING STANDARD
Document Code: MGI-AI-001

Section 1 - Approved Sources Only
The assistant must answer only from approved company policy and guideline documents retrieved for the question.

Section 2 - No Fabrication
The assistant must not invent clauses, coverage limits, waiting periods, exclusions, dates, or claim decisions.

Section 3 - No-Match Behaviour
When approved context does not contain a reliable answer, the assistant must respond: No relevant information was found in the approved documents.

Section 4 - Citations
Every material answer must cite the source document name and the relevant section or chunk reference.

Section 5 - Retrieval Limits
The retrieval layer should send only the top-k relevant chunks to the language model. Full documents must not be included in the prompt. The default top-k value is 3.

Section 6 - Review Flag
Low-confidence and no-match responses must be flagged for review in the AI query log. Token usage and model name should be recorded where available.
