MERIDIAN GENERAL INSURANCE
HEALTH HOSPITALIZATION POLICY
Document Code: MGI-HEALTH-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026

Section 1 - Inpatient Coverage
The policy covers medically necessary inpatient hospitalization for illness or accidental injury when admission exceeds 24 consecutive hours. Covered expenses include room charges, nursing, surgeon fees, anaesthesia, diagnostic tests, prescribed medicines, and operation-theatre charges, subject to the coverage limit.

Section 2 - Day-Care Procedures
Listed day-care procedures that require less than 24 hours because of modern medical technology are eligible when performed at a registered hospital.

Section 3 - Pre- and Post-Hospitalization
Eligible medical expenses incurred during the 30 days before admission and 60 days after discharge are covered when related to the same approved hospitalization.

Section 4 - Waiting Period
Pre-existing diseases are subject to a waiting period of 24 months from the policy start date unless the policy schedule provides a waiver.

Section 5 - Required Documents
The claimant must submit the claim form, hospital discharge summary, final hospital bill, itemized bill, payment receipts, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, identity proof, and bank details.

Section 6 - Exclusions
Cosmetic treatment without medical necessity, experimental treatment, self-inflicted injury, non-prescribed supplements, and treatment at an unregistered facility are excluded.
