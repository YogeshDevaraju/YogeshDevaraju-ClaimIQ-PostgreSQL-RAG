MERIDIAN GENERAL INSURANCE
INTERNAL CLAIMS PROCESSING GUIDELINE
Document Code: MGI-CLAIMS-GDL-001
Approved for Internal Use

Section 1 - Workflow
Every claim must follow: Submitted, Under Review, Approved or Rejected, and Closed. A claim cannot move directly from Submitted to Closed. A manager override requires written justification.

Section 2 - Validation
Before acceptance, confirm that required fields are present, the policy exists, the policy is active on the incident date, the claim type matches the policy type, and the claimed amount is positive.

Section 3 - Assignment
Claims should be assigned to an active Claims Adjuster. Adjusters may process claims assigned to them. Managers may reassign work when workload or regional coverage requires it.

Section 4 - Decision Notes
Approval or rejection must include a concise note describing the policy clause, evidence considered, and decision rationale. Sensitive identity numbers must not be written into notes or logs.

Section 5 - High-Value Review
Claims above INR 500,000 require Claims Manager review before approval.

Section 6 - Closure
A claim may be closed only after the approval or rejection decision, required communication, and supporting notes have been recorded.
