MERIDIAN GENERAL INSURANCE
PROPERTY PROTECTION POLICY
Document Code: MGI-PROPERTY-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 01 January 2026

Section 1 - Insured Property
The policy covers the insured building and declared contents at the address stated in the schedule.

Section 2 - Covered Events
Covered events include fire, lightning, explosion, storm, cyclone, flood, earthquake, impact by vehicles, riot, malicious damage, and burglary involving visible forcible entry.

Section 3 - Claim Requirements
The insured must submit the claim form, policy number, incident description, photographs or video, repair or replacement estimates, ownership evidence, inventory of damaged items, and fire-service or police report when applicable.

Section 4 - Loss Mitigation
The insured must take reasonable steps to prevent further damage, including emergency repairs. Permanent repairs require insurer approval unless needed for immediate safety.

Section 5 - Exclusions
Gradual deterioration, corrosion, defective workmanship, pests, unexplained disappearance, war, nuclear risk, and intentional loss are excluded.

Section 6 - Underinsurance
If the declared sum insured is lower than the replacement value, claim payment may be reduced proportionately under the average clause.
