MERIDIAN GENERAL INSURANCE
POLICY DOCUMENT GOVERNANCE STANDARD
Document Code: MGI-DOC-001

Section 1 - Approval
Only documents approved by an Admin may be used as sources by the ClaimIQ assistant. Draft, expired, inactive, or deleted documents must not be retrieved.

Section 2 - Upload Metadata
Each uploaded document must record its name, source type, uploader, upload timestamp, active status, and version where available.

Section 3 - Processing
Approved documents are extracted into text and divided into overlapping chunks. Each chunk must retain a reference to the source document and a section or chunk number.

Section 4 - Deactivation
When a document is deactivated, future AI answers must not use its chunks. Existing query logs remain unchanged for audit purposes.

Section 5 - Data Protection
Passwords, access tokens, full identity numbers, and unnecessary personal information must not be stored in document metadata, prompts, or logs.
